Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I cancel my membership anytime? ▾
Yes — all plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime with 7 days notice before your next billing date. No cancellation fees whatsoever.
Is there a joining or registration fee? ▾
No joining fee for Starter. Pro and Elite have a one-time PKR 500 registration fee covering your member card and initial fitness assessment.
Can I freeze my membership if I travel? ▾
Yes. Pro and Elite members can freeze for up to 30 days per year at no cost. Starter members can freeze for up to 15 days.
How do I book personal training sessions? ▾
Sessions can be booked via WhatsApp, the contact page, or in person at the front desk. We recommend booking 48 hours in advance for your preferred time slot.
Is there a free trial before committing? ▾
Absolutely. All new members receive a 3-day free trial to experience the full gym before choosing a membership plan. Contact us to claim yours.
