MAXIMIZE YOUR RESULTS

📊

TRACK CONSISTENTLY

Data beats feelings. Log every session — even bad ones. Patterns emerge after 3–4 weeks and tell you precisely what's working.

💧

HYDRATION FIRST

A 2% drop in hydration reduces performance by 10–20%. Aim for 35ml per kg of bodyweight daily, more on training days.

😴

SLEEP = GAINS

Muscle is built during sleep, not training. 7–9 hours triggers peak growth hormone release. Shortchange sleep, shortchange results.

🥗

PROTEIN PRIORITY

Aim for 1.6–2.2g of protein per kg of bodyweight daily. Hit your protein target first, then fill remaining calories with carbs and fats.
