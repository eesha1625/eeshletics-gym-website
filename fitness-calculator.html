Understanding Your Numbers

WHAT DO THESE NUMBERS MEAN?

BMI RANGES

Body Mass Index is a general indicator of body composition based on height and weight ratio.

UnderweightBelow 18.5
Normal18.5 – 24.9
Overweight25 – 29.9
Obese30 and above
BMR FORMULA

We use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the most clinically validated formula for resting calorie estimation.

Male(10×W)+(6.25×H)–(5×A)+5
Female(10×W)+(6.25×H)–(5×A)–161

W = weight (kg) · H = height (cm) · A = age (yr)

BODY TYPES

Your somatotype gives insight into how your body responds to food and training stimuli.

EctomorphLean, fast metabolism
MesomorphAthletic, responds fast
